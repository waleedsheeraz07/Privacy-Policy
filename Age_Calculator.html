📅 Privacy Policy

Age Calculator  •  Effective Date: 03/04/2026

Age Calculator is a simple utility app that calculates your exact age in years, months, and days based on your date of birth. Enter your birth date, and the app instantly shows how much time has passed — down to the day.

🔒 Your date of birth stays on your device. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information — including your birth date or age.

Information Collection and Use

Age Calculator does not collect, store, or transmit any personal or non-personal data. The date of birth you enter is used only for the immediate calculation and is never saved to any storage, database, or server. Each time you open the app, you can enter your birth date again — no data persists between sessions.

  • No personal data (name, email, location, birth date) is collected
  • No age calculation history is stored
  • No analytics or crash reporting tools are used
  • No internet connection is required or used

App Permissions

Age Calculator requires no special permissions to function. It does not access your storage, camera, microphone, contacts, location, or any other system features. You can install and use the app immediately without granting any permissions.

Offline Functionality

The app works completely offline. No internet connection is ever required — all age calculations are performed locally on your device using the device's system clock. The app never connects to external servers, APIs, or cloud services.

Third-Party Services

Age Calculator contains no third‑party SDKs, advertisements, analytics providers, or tracking libraries. There are no ads, no in-app purchases, and no external dependencies. Everything is self‑contained to guarantee your privacy.

Children’s Privacy

Since no data is collected from any users, Age Calculator fully complies with children’s data protection regulations such as COPPA, GDPR‑K, and other global privacy laws. The app is safe for all ages.

Data Security

Because no information is collected, stored, or transmitted, there is zero risk of unauthorized access, data breaches, or misuse. Your date of birth and age remain yours alone — they never leave your device.

Data Retention

Age Calculator does not retain any user data. The date of birth you enter is stored temporarily in the app's memory only while the app is open. Once you close the app, all entered information is discarded. No data is saved to the device's storage, and no data is retained by the application or the developer.

💡 No persistent storage: Each time you open Age Calculator, you start fresh. The app does not remember your previous birth date or age.

Data Deletion

Since the app never stores any user data, there is no user data to delete. Simply closing the app removes any temporary information from memory. If you uninstall the app, all associated temporary data is automatically removed. No further action is required.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the app’s functionality changes. Any updates will be reflected on this page with a revised effective date. We encourage you to review it occasionally, though the zero‑data commitment will always remain.
