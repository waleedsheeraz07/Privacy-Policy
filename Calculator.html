Privacy Policy

Calculator  •  Effective Date: 01/04/2026

Calculator is a straightforward utility app that provides essential arithmetic functions: addition, subtraction, multiplication, division, and percentage calculations. All operations are performed locally on your device with no external connections.

✅ This application operates entirely offline and does NOT collect, store, transmit, or share any user data.

Information Collection and Use

Calculator requires no user registration, account creation, or personal details. Every calculation stays on your device and is never transmitted or saved.

  • No personal data is collected
  • No calculation history is stored
  • No analytics or tracking tools are used
  • No internet connection is required or used

App Permissions

Calculator is built with a strict privacy-first approach and does not request or require any special permissions on your device. The app functions without accessing storage, camera, location, contacts, or any other system features. Because no data ever leaves your device, the app operates with the smallest possible permission footprint.

Offline Functionality

The app works completely offline and does not connect to any external servers, APIs, or cloud services. You can use it in airplane mode, without Wi‑Fi, or anywhere with no cellular signal.

Third-Party Services

Calculator contains no third‑party SDKs, advertisements, analytics providers, or tracking libraries. Everything is self‑contained to guarantee your privacy.

Children’s Privacy

Since no data is collected from any users, Calculator fully complies with children’s data protection regulations such as COPPA and GDPR‑K without any additional configuration.

Data Security

As no information is collected, stored, or transmitted, there is zero risk of unauthorized access, data breaches, or misuse. Your numbers remain yours alone.

Data Retention

Calculator does not collect, store, or retain any personal or non-personal user data. All calculations are performed live on your device and are never saved — not to storage, not to the app’s memory after closing, and not to any server. No data is retained by the application or the developer at any time.

Data Deletion

Because Calculator never stores any user data, there is no user data to delete. If you uninstall the app, any temporary calculation data shown on the screen is automatically removed from your device. No further action is required — your privacy is fully protected from installation to uninstallation.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the app’s functionality changes. Any updates will be reflected on this page with a revised effective date. We encourage you to review it occasionally, though the zero‑data commitment will always remain.
