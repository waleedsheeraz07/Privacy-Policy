Privacy Policy

🧮 Calculator • Simple & Secure
Effective Date: April 1, 2026

✅ Zero data collection — Your calculations never leave your device. No internet required, no tracking, no hidden permissions.

📱 App Overview

Calculator is a straightforward, no‑frills utility that lets you perform basic arithmetic: addition, subtraction, multiplication, division, and percentage calculations. Designed for speed, privacy, and offline use.

🔍 Information Collection and Use

Calculator does not collect, store, or transmit any personal or non‑personal information. There is:

  • No user accounts, logins, or profiles.
  • No analytics, crash reporters, or tracking tools.
  • No storage of calculation history (the app does not save previous results).
  • No internet connection — all operations happen locally on your device.

Your privacy is absolute: we never see what numbers you type or the results you get.

🔐 App Permissions

Calculator is built with a "minimum permission" philosophy. It requests zero permissions on your device. It does not access:

  • Storage / files
  • Camera or microphone
  • Location (GPS)
  • Contacts or phone state
  • Any other system features

Because the app never requires internet access or data storage, no special permissions are ever needed. Install and use immediately — no popups, no consent screens.

🌐 Offline Functionality

Calculator works 100% offline. It never attempts to connect to any external servers, APIs, or cloud services. You can use it in airplane mode, without Wi‑Fi, or in areas with no cellular signal.

📦 Third-Party Services

The app contains no third‑party SDKs, advertisements, or tracking libraries. It does not use Google Analytics, Firebase, AdMob, or any similar services. Everything is self‑contained and private by design.

🧒 Children’s Privacy

Calculator does not collect any data from anyone, including children under 13. Because there is no data collection, the app fully complies with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar global regulations without any additional configuration.

🛡️ Data Security

Since Calculator does not collect, store, or transmit any data, there is zero risk of data breaches, unauthorized access, or accidental leaks. No servers, no cloud backups, no hidden databases — your numbers remain yours alone.

🗄️ Data Retention

Calculator does not collect, store, or retain any personal or non-personal user data. All calculations are performed live on your device and are never saved — not to internal storage, not to the app’s memory after closing, and not to any external server. The app has no database and no history feature. Therefore, there is no data retained by the application or the developer.

🗑️ Data Deletion

Because Calculator never stores any user data, there is nothing to delete. If you uninstall the app, any ephemeral data (such as the current display value) is automatically removed from your device. No further action is required. You can be confident that your privacy is fully protected from the moment you install to long after you uninstall.

📝 Changes to This Privacy Policy

If the functionality of Calculator ever changes in a way that affects privacy, we will update this page accordingly. The “Effective Date” at the top will reflect the latest revision. We encourage you to review this policy occasionally, though rest assured — any future updates will maintain the same zero‑data commitment.
