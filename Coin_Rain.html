💰 Coin Rain

Privacy Policy  •  Effective Date: 30/05/2026

Coin Rain is a fast‑paced arcade game where you slide a basket to catch falling coins and avoid bombs. No accounts, no trackers, no hidden data collection – just private, addictive fun.

🔒 Zero data collection – Coin Rain does not collect, store, or share any personal or usage information. Everything stays on your device.

Information Collection and Use

Coin Rain is completely offline and does not collect:

  • Personal information (name, email, location, IP address)
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Gameplay analytics or crash reports
  • Any data sent to external servers

Local Data Storage

The app stores only one piece of information locally on your device:

  • Your highest score – saved using Android SharedPreferences

This data is used solely to display your personal best on the game over screen. It never leaves your device and is automatically deleted when you uninstall the app.

Background Music

The app includes a looping background music file stored inside the app package. No streaming, no downloads, no external connections. The music plays locally and stops when the app is paused or closed.

App Permissions

Coin Rain requires no special permissions. It does not request access to storage, location, contacts, camera, microphone, or internet.

Third‑Party Services

This app contains no advertisements, no analytics, and no third‑party SDKs (such as Firebase, AdMob, or Facebook). Everything is self‑contained in the installed application.

Children’s Privacy

Because Coin Rain collects no personal data, it is safe for users of all ages. The app complies with the principles of COPPA (Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act) and GDPR.

Data Security

With no data collected, there is no risk of data breaches, leaks, or unauthorised access. Your high score is stored in a private app‑only directory that no other app can read.

Data Deletion

You can delete all locally stored data (your high score) in two ways:

  • Uninstall the app – everything is removed.
  • Clear app data from Android settings → Apps → Coin Rain → Storage → Clear data.
🎮 Gameplay reminder: Slide your finger left/right to move the basket. Catch gold coins to increase your score. Avoid red bombs – they end the game! The game gets faster as you score higher.

This policy may be updated if new features or data handling practices are introduced in future versions. The latest version will always be available in the app’s listing or on request.
