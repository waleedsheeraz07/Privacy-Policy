🏀 Privacy Policy

Ball Bounce  •  Effective Date: 09/04/2026

Ball Bounce is a simple arcade game designed for quick reflexes and focused gameplay. No accounts. No interruptions. Just pure skill.

🔒 This app does not collect, store, or share any personal data. Everything runs entirely on your device.

Information Collection and Use

Ball Bounce does not collect any personal or user data.

  • No personal information is collected
  • No gameplay data is sent to any server
  • No tracking or analytics tools are used
  • No internet connection is required

Local Gameplay Data

The app stores minimal data locally on your device, including:

  • Your best score (for high score tracking)

This data is stored only on your device using local storage and is never shared externally.

Gameplay

Tap to bounce the ball and keep it on the moving platform. The longer you survive and the more accurate your timing, the higher your score.

App Permissions

Ball Bounce requires no special permissions. It does not access your storage, location, contacts, camera, or microphone.

Third-Party Services

This app does not use advertisements, analytics, or any third-party services.

Children’s Privacy

Since no personal data is collected, Ball Bounce is safe for users of all ages and aligns with privacy-friendly standards such as GDPR and COPPA principles.

Data Security

With no data collection, there is no risk of data misuse, leaks, or unauthorized access.

Data Retention

Only your best score is stored locally. Uninstalling the app removes this data completely.

🏀 Simple truth: You tap. The ball bounces. Nothing leaves your device.

Data Deletion

You can clear all stored data by uninstalling the app, which removes your best score instantly.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This policy may be updated if new features are added in future versions.
