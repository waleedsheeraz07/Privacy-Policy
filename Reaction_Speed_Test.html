⚡ Privacy Policy

Reaction Speed Test  •  Effective Date: 04/04/2026

Reaction Speed Test is a minimal, reflex-based game built for one purpose: to measure how fast you react. No distractions. No tracking. Just pure speed.

🔒 This app does not collect, store, or share any personal data. Everything runs locally on your device.

Information Collection and Use

Reaction Speed Test does not collect any personal or usage data.

  • No personal information is collected
  • No gameplay data is sent or stored online
  • No analytics or tracking tools are used
  • No internet connection is required

Local Data Storage

The app may store your best score within the current session only to enhance gameplay experience.

  • This data never leaves your device
  • It is not shared or transmitted
  • It resets when the app is closed

Gameplay

When you start the game, the screen shows a red “Wait” signal. Once it turns green, you tap as quickly as possible. Your reaction time is calculated instantly on your device.

App Permissions

Reaction Speed Test requires no special permissions. It does not access your storage, location, contacts, camera, or microphone.

Third-Party Services

This app does not use advertisements, analytics platforms, or any third-party services.

Children’s Privacy

Since no data is collected, this app is safe for users of all ages and aligns with privacy-friendly standards such as GDPR and COPPA principles.

Data Security

With zero data collection, there is no risk of data misuse, leakage, or external access.

Data Retention

No personal data is retained. Session-based scores are temporary and cleared automatically.

⚡ Simple truth: You tap. The app measures. Nothing else happens.

Data Deletion

Since no data is stored, there is nothing to delete. Closing or uninstalling the app removes everything instantly.
