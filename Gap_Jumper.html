🟦 Privacy Policy

Gap Jumper  •  Effective Date: 17/04/2026

Gap Jumper is a fast‑paced, reflex‑based arcade game. You control a square that moves forward automatically — tap to jump over gaps in the ground, collect power‑ups (double jump, slow motion), and beat your own best score. The app is designed with your privacy as the highest priority: no tracking, no data collection, no internet required.

🔒 Zero data collection — This app never collects, stores, or shares any personal or gameplay information externally.

Information Collection and Use

Gap Jumper does not collect any data. There is no analytics SDK, no crash reporting tool, no advertising framework, and no internet permission.

  • No personal information (name, email, device ID, location, etc.) is collected.
  • Your in‑game score and best score are saved only locally on your device using Android SharedPreferences.
  • No scores or gameplay data are ever sent to any server or third party.
  • No network access is required or used — the game works completely offline.

Local Storage (SharedPreferences)

The game stores one piece of data on your device: your best score (the highest number of gaps you have successfully jumped over in a single run). This data stays on your device and is only used to display your personal best. Uninstalling the app removes this data automatically.

Gameplay Overview

A blue square moves forward automatically. You tap the screen to make it jump. The ground contains gaps — jump over them to survive. Each gap you clear adds 1 point to your score. As your score increases, the game speeds up and gaps appear more frequently. Rare power‑ups appear: Double Jump (allows a second jump in mid‑air) and Slow Motion (temporarily reduces the game speed by 60%).

All game logic (physics, collision detection, speed scaling, power‑up effects) runs locally on your device. No information about your performance or interactions leaves your device.

App Permissions

Gap Jumper requires no special permissions. It does not access the internet, storage (beyond its own private SharedPreferences), camera, microphone, contacts, or location.

Third-Party Services

This app uses no third‑party libraries that collect data. No ads, no analytics, no crash reporters. The only external code is the standard Android SDK and AppCompat (both required to run the app).

Children’s Privacy

Because no data is collected in any form, Gap Jumper is safe for children of all ages. It fully complies with COPPA, GDPR‑K, and other child privacy regulations.

Data Security

With zero data transmission or storage outside your device, there is no risk of data breaches, leaks, or unauthorised access. Your best score is stored using Android’s standard secure local storage, accessible only by the app itself.

Data Retention and Deletion

No data is retained beyond your device’s local storage. To delete all app‑related data (including your best score):

  • Go to Android Settings → Apps → Gap Jumper → Storage → Clear Data, or
  • Simply uninstall the app.

After uninstallation, no trace of your gameplay remains.
