⌨️ Privacy Policy

TypeRush  •  Effective Date: 07/04/2026

TypeRush is a fast-paced typing challenge designed to sharpen your speed and accuracy. No accounts. No tracking. Just you versus time.

🔒 This app does not collect, store, or share any personal data. Everything runs entirely on your device.

Information Collection and Use

TypeRush does not collect any personal or usage data.

  • No personal information is collected
  • No typing data is transmitted or stored online
  • No analytics or tracking tools are used
  • No internet connection is required

Local Data Processing

All gameplay metrics are calculated instantly on your device, including:

  • Words Per Minute (WPM)
  • Accuracy (ACC)
  • Characters typed
  • Characters correct

This data is temporary and session-based only.

Gameplay

When you press Start Test, a random paragraph appears with a 30-second timer. You type as quickly and accurately as possible. Once the timer ends—or you finish early— your results are calculated instantly.

App Permissions

TypeRush requires no special permissions. It does not access your storage, location, contacts, camera, or microphone.

Third-Party Services

This app does not use advertisements, analytics tools, or any third-party services.

Children’s Privacy

Since no data is collected, TypeRush is safe for users of all ages and aligns with privacy-friendly standards such as GDPR and COPPA principles.

Data Security

With zero data collection, there is no risk of data misuse, leakage, or unauthorized access.

Data Retention

No personal data is retained. All typing results exist only during the active session and are cleared automatically.

⌨️ Simple truth: You type. The app measures. Nothing leaves your device.

Data Deletion

Since no data is stored, there is nothing to delete. Closing or uninstalling the app removes everything instantly.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

If new features are introduced in future updates, this policy may be updated accordingly.
