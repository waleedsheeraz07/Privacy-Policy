Privacy Policy

Salary Breakdown Calculator  •  Effective Date: 01/04/2026

Salary Breakdown Calculator is a simple utility app that converts between yearly, monthly, weekly, and daily salary figures. Enter any value (yearly, monthly, weekly, or daily) and the app instantly calculates the corresponding amounts for all other time periods.

  • Yearly Salary
  • Monthly Salary
  • Weekly Salary
  • Daily Salary
🔒 This application operates entirely offline and does NOT collect, store, transmit, or share any user data. All conversions happen locally on your device.

Information Collection and Use

Salary Breakdown Calculator does not require user registration, account creation, or any personal details. Every salary value you enter stays on your device, is used only for the immediate calculation, and is never saved or transmitted.

  • No personal data is collected
  • No salary history is stored
  • No analytics or tracking tools are used
  • No internet connection is required or used

App Permissions

The app is built with privacy as the top priority and does not request or require any special permissions on your device. It does not access storage, camera, location, contacts, or any other system features. Because no data leaves your device, the app operates with the smallest possible permission footprint — ensuring your privacy remains intact.

Offline Functionality

Salary Breakdown Calculator works completely offline. It never connects to external servers, APIs, or cloud services. You can use it in airplane mode, without Wi‑Fi, or anywhere without cellular data.

Third-Party Services

The app contains no third‑party SDKs, advertisements, analytics providers, or tracking libraries. Everything is self‑contained to guarantee your privacy.

Children’s Privacy

Since no data is collected from any users, Salary Breakdown Calculator fully complies with children’s data protection regulations such as COPPA, GDPR‑K, and other global privacy laws.

Data Security

As no information is collected, stored, or transmitted, there is zero risk of unauthorized access, data breaches, or misuse. Your salary figures remain yours alone — they never leave your device.

Data Retention

Salary Breakdown Calculator does not collect, store, or retain any personal or non-personal user data. All conversions are performed live on your device and are never saved — not to storage, not to the app’s memory after closing, and not to any server. Even the “CLEAR ALL” function simply resets the input fields; no data was ever retained to begin with.

Data Deletion

Because the app never stores any user data, there is no user data to delete. If you uninstall the app, all temporary values shown on the screen are automatically removed from your device. No further action is required — your privacy is fully protected from installation to uninstallation.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the app’s functionality changes. Any updates will be reflected on this page with a revised effective date. We encourage you to review it occasionally, though the zero‑data commitment will always remain.
