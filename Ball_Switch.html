🎨 Ball Switch

Privacy Policy  •  Effective Date: 25/05/2026

Ball Switch is a reflex‑based color‑matching arcade game. You control a ball that changes color, swipe to switch lanes, and pass through gates of matching colors. No accounts, no trackers, no hidden data collection – just fast, private fun.

🔒 Zero data collection – Ball Switch does not collect, store, or share any personal or usage information. Everything stays on your device.

Information Collection and Use

Ball Switch is completely offline and does not collect:

  • Personal information (name, email, location, IP address)
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Gameplay analytics or crash reports
  • Any data sent to external servers

Local Data Storage

The app stores only one piece of information locally on your device:

  • Your highest score – saved using Android SharedPreferences

This data is used solely to display your personal best on the game over screen. It never leaves your device and is automatically deleted when you uninstall the app.

Background Music

The app includes a looping background music file (10‑minute track) stored inside the app package. No streaming, no downloads, no external connections. The music plays locally and stops when the app is paused or closed.

App Permissions

Ball Switch requires no special permissions. It does not request access to storage, location, contacts, camera, microphone, or internet.

Third‑Party Services

This app contains no advertisements, no analytics, and no third‑party SDKs (such as Firebase, AdMob, or Facebook). Everything is self‑contained in the installed application.

Children’s Privacy

Because Ball Switch collects no personal data, it is safe for users of all ages. The app complies with the principles of COPPA (Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act) and GDPR.

Data Security

With no data collected, there is no risk of data breaches, leaks, or unauthorised access. Your high score is stored in a private app‑only directory that no other app can read.

Data Deletion

You can delete all locally stored data (your high score) in two ways:

  • Uninstall the app – everything is removed.
  • Clear app data from Android settings → Apps → Ball Switch → Storage → Clear data.
🎮 Gameplay reminder: Swipe left/right to change lanes. Match the ball color with the gate color to pass. The faster you match, the higher your score climbs!
