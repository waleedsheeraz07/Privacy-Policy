Privacy Policy

Speed Unit Converter  •  Effective Date: 11/04/2026

Speed Unit Converter is a lightweight utility application designed to instantly convert between different units of speed. You can enter a value and switch between units such as kilometers per hour (km/h), meters per second (m/s), miles per hour (mph), and knots, and the result is calculated immediately in real time.

🔒 This application operates fully offline. It does NOT collect, store, transmit, or share any personal or non-personal data. Every calculation happens locally on your device without any internet connection.

Information Collection and Use

Speed Unit Converter does not require registration, login, or any form of personal identification. All values entered by the user remain strictly on the device and are used only for instant conversion.

  • No personal data is collected or stored
  • No conversion history is saved
  • No analytics or tracking systems are used
  • No internet connection is required at any time

App Permissions

This application does not request any device permissions. It does not access storage, location, camera, contacts, or any system-level data. The app is designed to operate independently with zero intrusion into user privacy.

Offline Functionality

All conversions are performed entirely offline. The app does not connect to servers, APIs, or cloud services. It can be used anytime, anywhere—even without Wi-Fi or mobile data.

Third-Party Services

Speed Unit Converter does not include any third-party SDKs, advertising modules, analytics tools, or external integrations. It is completely self-contained and free from external tracking technologies.

Children’s Privacy

Since no data is collected or processed externally, this app is safe for users of all ages and complies with global child privacy standards.

Data Security

Because no information is stored or transmitted, there is no risk of data leakage, misuse, or unauthorized access. Everything remains on the user’s device and disappears when the app is closed.

Data Retention

Speed Unit Converter does not retain any data whatsoever. No inputs, outputs, or usage history are saved in memory or storage. All calculations exist only during active use and are immediately discarded afterward.

Data Deletion

As no data is ever stored, there is nothing to delete. Uninstalling the app completely removes all temporary runtime values automatically. No additional steps are required from the user.

Changes to This Privacy Policy
