💀 TAP OR DIE

Privacy Policy  •  Effective Date: 27/04/2026

Tap or Die is a fast‑paced reflex game. Three circles appear on screen — one green, two red. Tap the green circle before the timer runs out to earn points and keep playing. Tap a red circle, miss completely, or let the timer hit zero, and it's game over. As your score increases, the allowed time shrinks, and the background changes color. The game is designed to be completely private — no tracking, no data collection, no internet required.

🛡️ Zero data collection — This app never collects, stores, or shares any personal or gameplay information externally. Your high score stays only on your device.

Information Collection and Use

Tap or Die does not collect any data. There are no analytics SDKs, no crash reporting tools, no advertising frameworks, and no internet permission. The app works fully offline.

  • No personal information (name, email, device ID, location, etc.) is collected.
  • Your current score and best (high) score are saved only locally on your device using Android SharedPreferences.
  • No scores, gameplay data, or any other information are ever sent to any server or third party.
  • No network access is required or used — the game runs 100% offline.

Local Storage (SharedPreferences)

The game stores exactly one piece of data on your device: your best score (the highest number of green circles you have successfully tapped before dying). This data is only used to display your personal high score in the game over screen. Uninstalling the app removes this data automatically.

Gameplay Overview

When you start a new game, three circles appear at random positions on the screen (spaced apart). One circle is bright GREEN — tapping it gives you +1 point, makes the game slightly faster (time limit reduces gradually), and spawns a fresh set of circles. The other two circles are RED — tapping any red circle ends the game immediately. If you tap anywhere outside all circles, the game also ends.

Each time you tap the green circle correctly:

  • Your score increases by 1.
  • The remaining time for the next round becomes slightly shorter (starting at 3 seconds, down to a minimum of 0.7 seconds).
  • The background color may change to a new exciting hue every 8 points.
  • A satisfying particle effect and screen flash occur.

All game logic (positions, timing, difficulty scaling, visual effects) runs locally on your device. No information about your taps, speed, or performance leaves your device.

App Permissions

Tap or Die requires no special permissions. It does not access the internet, storage (beyond its own private SharedPreferences for high score), camera, microphone, contacts, or location. The game only draws on the screen and registers touch events.

Third-Party Services

This app uses no third‑party libraries that collect data. No ads, no analytics, no crash reporters. The only external code is the standard Android SDK and AppCompat (both required to run the app).

Children’s Privacy

Because no data is collected in any form, Tap or Die is safe for children of all ages. It fully complies with COPPA, GDPR‑K, and other child privacy regulations. The game contains no in‑app purchases, no chat, no user‑generated content, and no external links.

Data Security

With zero data transmission or storage outside your device, there is no risk of data breaches, leaks, or unauthorised access. Your best score is stored using Android’s standard secure local storage (SharedPreferences), accessible only by the app itself.

Data Retention and Deletion

No data is retained beyond your device’s local storage. To delete all app‑related data (including your best score):

  • Go to Android Settings → Apps → Tap or Die → Storage → Clear Data, or
  • Simply uninstall the app.

After uninstallation, no trace of your gameplay remains.
