🦊 Privacy Policy

Fox Runner  •  Effective Date: 23/04/2026

Fox Runner is an exciting, reflex‑based runner game. You control a cute fox that runs automatically — tap to jump over red block obstacles, collect power‑ups (double jump, slow motion, shield), and beat your own high score. The app is designed with your privacy as the highest priority: no tracking, no data collection, no internet required.

🔒 Zero data collection — This app never collects, stores, or shares any personal or gameplay information externally.

Information Collection and Use

Fox Runner does not collect any data. There is no analytics SDK, no crash reporting tool, no advertising framework, and no internet permission.

  • No personal information (name, email, device ID, location, etc.) is collected.
  • Your in‑game score and best score are saved only locally on your device using Android SharedPreferences.
  • No scores or gameplay data are ever sent to any server or third party.
  • No network access is required or used — the game works completely offline.

Local Storage (SharedPreferences)

The game stores one piece of data on your device: your best score (the highest number of obstacles you have successfully passed in a single run). This data stays on your device and is only used to display your personal best. Uninstalling the app removes this data automatically.

Gameplay Overview

A cute fox runs automatically from left to right while red block obstacles scroll toward you. Tap the screen to make the fox jump. Avoid hitting obstacles — each obstacle you pass adds 1 point to your score. As your score increases, the game speeds up and obstacles appear more frequently. Rare power‑ups appear floating near the ground:

  • Double Jump – allows you to jump once more while in mid‑air.
  • Slow Mode – temporarily reduces the game speed by 40% (including the fox’s jump physics).
  • Shield – makes the fox invincible for a few seconds, allowing you to pass through obstacles safely.

All game logic (physics, collision detection, speed scaling, power‑up effects) runs locally on your device. No information about your performance or interactions leaves your device.

App Permissions

Fox Runner requires no special permissions. It does not access the internet, storage (beyond its own private SharedPreferences), camera, microphone, contacts, or location.

Third-Party Services

This app uses no third‑party libraries that collect data. No ads, no analytics, no crash reporters. The only external code is the standard Android SDK and AppCompat (both required to run the app).

Children’s Privacy

Because no data is collected in any form, Fox Runner is safe for children of all ages. It fully complies with COPPA, GDPR‑K, and other child privacy regulations.

Data Security

With zero data transmission or storage outside your device, there is no risk of data breaches, leaks, or unauthorised access. Your best score is stored using Android’s standard secure local storage, accessible only by the app itself.

Data Retention and Deletion

No data is retained beyond your device’s local storage. To delete all app‑related data (including your best score):

  • Go to Android Settings → Apps → Fox Runner → Storage → Clear Data, or
  • Simply uninstall the app.

After uninstallation, no trace of your gameplay remains.
