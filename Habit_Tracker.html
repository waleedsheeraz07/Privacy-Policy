📋 Privacy Policy

Habit Tracker  •  Effective Date: 02/04/2026

Habit Tracker is a simple, private app that helps you build and maintain daily habits. You can add habits, tap them once each day to increase your streak, and long‑press to delete them. Your habits and streak data are stored only on your device — never on any external server.

🔐 Your data stays on your device. We do not collect, view, or share any of your habits, streaks, or personal information.

Information Collection and Use

Habit Tracker does not collect, transmit, or share any personal or non-personal data. All habit names, completion statuses, streak counts, and timestamps are saved locally on your device using the app's internal storage (or your device’s local database). This data never leaves your phone.

  • No personal data (name, email, location) is collected
  • No usage analytics or crash reporting tools are used
  • No internet connection is required or used for habit tracking
  • No third-party SDKs or advertising networks

App Permissions

Habit Tracker requires no special permissions to function. It does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, location, or files. The app only uses local storage (internal app data) to save your habits — this is a standard system feature that does not require explicit storage permission on modern Android versions. Your privacy remains completely intact.

Local Storage – What and Why

The app stores the following information locally on your device:

  • Habit names you create
  • Current streak count for each habit
  • Last completion date (to reset streaks if a day is missed)

This data is stored only on your device and is never backed up to any external server or cloud service unless you manually choose to back up your device (e.g., via Google Drive). Even then, the developer has no access to this data.

Offline Functionality

Habit Tracker works completely offline. No internet connection is required to add habits, mark them as done, or view your streaks. The app never attempts to connect to any external servers, APIs, or cloud services.

Third-Party Services

The app contains no third‑party SDKs, advertisements, analytics providers, or tracking libraries. There are no ads, no in-app purchases, and no external dependencies. Everything is self‑contained to guarantee your privacy.

Children’s Privacy

Since no data is collected from any users, Habit Tracker fully complies with children’s data protection regulations such as COPPA, GDPR‑K, and other global privacy laws. The app is safe for all ages.

Data Security

Because all data stays on your device, there is no risk of data breaches or unauthorized access from external parties. Your habits are as secure as your device’s own file system. We recommend using device screen lock for additional protection.

Data Retention

Your habit data is retained locally on your device for as long as you keep the app installed and do not delete the habits. You can delete individual habits by long‑pressing them in the app. If you uninstall the app, all associated habit data is removed from your device automatically. The developer does not retain any copies of your data.

💡 User Control: You are in full control. Delete habits anytime via long‑press. Clear app data from device settings to remove everything at once. Uninstalling the app also removes all data.

Data Deletion

You can delete all your habit data in the following ways:

  • Delete individual habits: Long‑press on any habit to remove it immediately.
  • Clear app data: Go to your device's Settings → Apps → Habit Tracker → Storage → Clear Data. This removes all habits and streaks.
  • Uninstall the app: Uninstalling completely removes all stored habit data from your device.

Since no data is ever transmitted to any server, you do not need to contact the developer to delete anything — everything is under your control.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the app’s functionality changes. Any updates will be reflected on this page with a revised effective date. We encourage you to review it occasionally, though the privacy‑first commitment will always remain.
