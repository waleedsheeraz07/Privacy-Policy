Privacy Policy

Discount Calculator  •  Effective Date: 31/03/2026

Discount Calculator is a simple utility app that helps users calculate savings and final price by entering:

  • Original Price
  • Discount Percentage
This application operates entirely offline and does NOT collect, store, transmit, or share any user data.

Information Collection and Use

Discount Calculator does not require user registration, account creation, or personal details. All calculations are performed locally on the device and remain visible only to the user.

  • No personal data is collected
  • No financial information is stored
  • No analytics or tracking tools are used
  • No internet connection is required

App Permissions

Discount Calculator is designed to be a lightweight, privacy-first tool and does not request or require any special permissions on your device. The app functions without accessing storage, camera, location, contacts, or any other system features. Since all calculations happen locally and no data is transmitted, the app operates with the lowest possible permission footprint, ensuring your privacy remains completely intact.

Offline Functionality

The app works completely offline and does not connect to any external servers, APIs, or cloud services.

Third-Party Services

Discount Calculator does not use advertisements, analytics providers, or third-party SDKs.

Children’s Privacy

Since no data is collected from any users, Discount Calculator complies with children’s data protection regulations.

Data Security

As no information is collected or stored, there is no risk of unauthorized access, misuse, or data leakage.

Data Retention

Discount Calculator does not collect, store, or retain any personal or non-personal user data. All calculations are performed locally on the user's device and are not saved to the device, the app, or transmitted to any server. No data is stored at any time, so there is nothing retained by the application or the developer.

Data Deletion

Since Discount Calculator does not store or retain any data, there is no user data to delete. If a user uninstalls the app, all temporary calculation data used during that session is automatically removed from the device. Users do not need to take any further action to delete data because no data is ever stored.
