Privacy Policy

Distance Converter  •  Effective Date: 02/04/2026

Distance Converter is a simple utility app that instantly converts between different units of distance. Enter a numeric value, select the unit you're converting from (e.g., Kilometer, Meter, Mile, Yard, Foot, Inch, Centimeter, Millimeter), choose the unit you want to convert to, and the app displays the converted result immediately.

🔒 This application operates entirely offline and does NOT collect, store, transmit, or share any user data. All conversions happen locally on your device — no internet connection is ever required or used.

Information Collection and Use

Distance Converter does not require user registration, account creation, or any personal details. The distance values you enter stay on your device, are used only for the immediate conversion, and are never saved or transmitted anywhere.

  • No personal data is collected
  • No conversion history is stored
  • No analytics or tracking tools are used
  • No internet connection is required or used

App Permissions

The app is built with a strict privacy-first approach and does not request or require any special permissions on your device. It does not access storage, camera, location, contacts, or any other system features. Because no data ever leaves your device, the app operates with the smallest possible permission footprint — ensuring your privacy remains completely intact.

Offline Functionality

Distance Converter works completely offline. It never connects to external servers, APIs, or cloud services. You can use it in airplane mode, without Wi‑Fi, or anywhere with no cellular signal. All conversion logic is built directly into the app.

Third-Party Services

The app contains no third‑party SDKs, advertisements, analytics providers, or tracking libraries. There are no ads, no in-app purchases, and no external dependencies. Everything is self‑contained to guarantee your privacy.

Children’s Privacy

Since no data is collected from any users, Distance Converter fully complies with children’s data protection regulations such as COPPA, GDPR‑K, and other global privacy laws. The app is safe for all ages.

Data Security

As no information is collected, stored, or transmitted, there is zero risk of unauthorized access, data breaches, or misuse. Your distance values remain yours alone — they never leave your device.

Data Retention

Distance Converter does not collect, store, or retain any personal or non-personal user data. All conversions are performed live on your device and are never saved — not to storage, not to the app’s memory after closing, and not to any server. No data is retained by the application or the developer at any time.

Data Deletion

Because the app never stores any user data, there is no user data to delete. If you uninstall the app, all temporary values shown on the screen are automatically removed from your device. No further action is required — your privacy is fully protected from installation to uninstallation.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the app’s functionality changes. Any updates will be reflected on this page with a revised effective date. We encourage you to review it occasionally, though the zero‑data commitment will always remain.
