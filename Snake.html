🐍 Privacy Policy

Snake  •  Effective Date: 07/04/2026

Snake is a simple, offline arcade game focused on reflexes and focus. No accounts. No distractions. Just the classic experience.

🔒 This app does not collect, store, or share any personal data. Everything runs entirely on your device.

Information Collection and Use

Snake does not collect any personal or usage data.

  • No personal information is collected
  • No gameplay data is transmitted or stored online
  • No analytics or tracking tools are used
  • No internet connection is required

Local Gameplay Data

All game activity is processed locally on your device, including:

  • Your score
  • Snake movement and game state

This data is temporary and exists only during gameplay.

Gameplay

You control the snake using swipe gestures. The objective is to collect apples, grow longer, and avoid collisions with walls or yourself.

App Permissions

Snake requires no special permissions. It does not access your storage, location, contacts, camera, or microphone.

Third-Party Services

This app does not use advertisements, analytics tools, or any third-party services.

Children’s Privacy

Since no data is collected, Snake is safe for users of all ages and aligns with privacy-friendly standards such as GDPR and COPPA principles.

Data Security

With zero data collection, there is no risk of data misuse, leakage, or unauthorized access.

Data Retention

No personal data is retained. All gameplay data is temporary and cleared automatically.

🐍 Simple truth: You play. The snake moves. Nothing leaves your device.

Data Deletion

Since no data is stored, there is nothing to delete. Closing or uninstalling the app removes everything instantly.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

If new features are introduced in future updates, this policy may be updated accordingly.
