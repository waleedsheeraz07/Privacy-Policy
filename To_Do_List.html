📋 Privacy Policy

To Do List  •  Effective Date: 11/04/2026

To Do List is a simple productivity app that helps you create tasks, mark them as completed, and delete them when finished. The app is built with a privacy-first mindset — everything stays entirely on your device.

🔐 No data is collected. No data is shared. Everything stays on your device.

Information Collection and Use

To Do List does not collect, store, or transmit any personal or non-personal data. All tasks you create, completion statuses, and app interactions exist only locally on your device.

  • No personal data (name, email, phone number, location)
  • No analytics or tracking
  • No internet communication
  • No third-party integrations or SDKs

App Permissions

This app requires no special permissions. It does not access your camera, contacts, storage, or any sensitive system features. It operates entirely within its own local environment.

Local Data Storage

The app stores the following information locally:

  • Task names you create
  • Completion status of each task

This data is saved only on your device and is never transmitted anywhere. The developer has zero access to your data.

Offline Functionality

To Do List works completely offline. You can add, complete, and delete tasks without any internet connection. The app does not connect to any servers or external services.

Third-Party Services

The app does not use any third-party services. There are no ads, no analytics tools, and no external dependencies.

Children’s Privacy

Since no data is collected, the app is safe for users of all ages and complies with global privacy standards.

Data Security

Your data is stored locally on your device, making it inherently secure. There is no risk of server breaches because no data is stored externally.

Data Retention

Your tasks remain on your device until you delete them or uninstall the app. Removing the app will permanently delete all stored data.

💡 You are in full control: Delete tasks anytime or uninstall the app to remove all data instantly.

Data Deletion

  • Delete tasks: Remove tasks directly within the app
  • Clear app data: Use your device settings
  • Uninstall: Removes all data permanently

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This policy may be updated if the app evolves. Any updates will be reflected here with a revised effective date.
