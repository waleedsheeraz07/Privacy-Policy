💧 Privacy Policy

Water Intake Counter  •  Effective Date: 03/04/2026

Water Intake Counter is a simple hydration tracker that helps you achieve your daily water drinking goals. You set a daily goal (e.g., 2000ml) and log your intake using buttons for 100ml, 250ml, and 500ml. A "Reset Today" button clears the day's progress. All your data stays on your device.

💧 Your hydration data stays on your device. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information — including your water intake or daily goals.

Information Collection and Use

Water Intake Counter does not collect, transmit, or share any personal or non-personal data. All information — your daily water goal, current intake progress, and completion status — is stored locally on your device using the app's internal storage (local storage). This data never leaves your phone.

  • No personal data (name, email, location, health data) is collected
  • No water intake history is transmitted anywhere
  • No analytics or crash reporting tools are used
  • No internet connection is required or used

App Permissions

Water Intake Counter requires no special permissions to function. It does not access your storage, camera, microphone, contacts, location, or any other system features. The app uses only local storage (internal app data) to save your goal and daily progress — this is a standard system feature that does not require explicit storage permission on modern Android versions.

Local Storage – What and Why

The app stores the following information locally on your device:

  • Your daily water intake goal (e.g., 2000ml)
  • Current day's logged water amount
  • Completion status (whether you reached your goal)

This data is stored only on your device and is never backed up to any external server or cloud service unless you manually choose to back up your device (e.g., via Google Drive). Even then, the developer has no access to this data.

Offline Functionality

The app works completely offline. No internet connection is required to set goals, log water intake, or reset the day. The app never connects to external servers, APIs, or cloud services.

Third-Party Services

Water Intake Counter contains no third‑party SDKs, advertisements, analytics providers, or tracking libraries. There are no ads, no in-app purchases, and no external dependencies. Everything is self‑contained to guarantee your privacy.

Children’s Privacy

Since no data is collected from any users, Water Intake Counter fully complies with children’s data protection regulations such as COPPA, GDPR‑K, and other global privacy laws. The app is safe for all ages.

Data Security

Because all data stays on your device, there is no risk of data breaches or unauthorized access from external parties. Your water intake information is as secure as your device’s own file system. We recommend using device screen lock for additional protection.

Data Retention

Your water goal and daily intake progress are retained locally on your device for as long as you keep the app installed. The "Reset Today" button clears only the current day's progress (not your goal). If you uninstall the app, all associated data (goal and history) is removed from your device automatically. The developer does not retain any copies of your data.

💡 User Control: You are in full control. Use "Reset Today" to clear daily progress. Clear app data from device settings to remove everything at once (including your goal). Uninstalling the app also removes all data.

Data Deletion

You can delete all your water intake data in the following ways:

  • Reset daily progress: Tap "Reset Today" to clear the current day's logged water (your goal remains).
  • Clear all data: Go to your device's Settings → Apps → Water Intake Counter → Storage → Clear Data. This removes your goal and all progress.
  • Uninstall the app: Uninstalling completely removes all stored data from your device.

Since no data is ever transmitted to any server, you do not need to contact the developer to delete anything — everything is under your control.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the app’s functionality changes. Any updates will be reflected on this page with a revised effective date. We encourage you to review it occasionally, though the privacy‑first commitment will always remain.
