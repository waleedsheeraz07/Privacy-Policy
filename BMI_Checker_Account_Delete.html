How to Request Account Deletion

Follow these simple steps to request permanent deletion of your account:

  1. Open your email client (Gmail, Outlook, etc.)
  2. Compose a new email to:
vijaygpc7@gmail.comdiv>

Use the subject line:

DELETE MY BMI CHECKER ACCOUNT
  1. Include the following information in your email:
    • The email address you used to sign up for BMI Checker
    • A brief statement: "I request permanent deletion of my BMI Checker account and all associated data."
  2. Send the email. You will receive a confirmation within 7 days.
  3. Once verified, your account and all associated data will be permanently deleted.
📧 Need help? If you have trouble sending the email or don't receive a confirmation within 7 days, please send a follow-up email or contact support at the same address.

Verification Process

To protect your privacy and prevent unauthorized deletion requests, we may ask you to verify that you are the account owner by:

  • Responding to the confirmation email from your registered email address
  • Providing additional identifying information if needed

Timeline

  • Confirmation email sent: Within 2 business days
  • Account deletion completed: Within 30 days of verification
  • Data permanently removed: Immediately upon deletion

Alternative Method

If you are unable to send an email, you can also request deletion by:

  • Contacting support through the Google Play Store listing page

However, email is the preferred and fastest method.
