🏓 Bounce Master

Privacy Policy  •  Effective Date: 02 June 2026

Bounce Master is a minimalist arcade reflex game. You control a paddle, bounce a ball, and survive as the speed gradually increases. No data is collected, stored, or shared – only your best score lives locally on your device.

🔒 Zero data collection – Bounce Master does not collect any personal information, device identifiers, analytics, or crash logs. Everything stays private on your phone.

Information Collection and Use

Bounce Master is completely offline and does not collect:

  • Personal information (name, email, address, IP, location)
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs (Google Advertising ID, IMEI, etc.)
  • Gameplay analytics, session duration, or crash reports
  • Any form of telemetry or server communication

Local Data Storage

The app stores only one piece of information locally on your device using Android’s SharedPreferences:

  • Your highest score – the best number of paddle hits you've achieved in a single game.

This data is used exclusively to show your personal best on the game over screen. It never leaves your device, is not accessible by any other app, and is deleted automatically when you uninstall Bounce Master.

No Internet or Permissions

Bounce Master requests no permissions whatsoever. It has no access to:

  • Internet (no network calls of any kind)
  • Storage, photos, or files
  • Location, camera, microphone, or contacts

Because the app never goes online, there is zero risk of data transmission or leakage.

No Third‑Party Services

This app contains no advertisements, no analytics SDKs, no crash reporting tools, and no third‑party libraries that collect data. Bounce Master is built entirely with standard Android SDK components and runs self‑contained.

Children’s Privacy

Bounce Master collects no personal data from anyone, regardless of age. The app complies fully with COPPA (Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act), GDPR, and other privacy regulations. It is safe for users of all ages.

Data Security

Since no data is collected, there is nothing to secure or leak. The local high score is stored in a private app directory that only Bounce Master can access – no other application can read or modify it.

Data Deletion

You can remove all local data (your saved high score) at any time:

  • Uninstall the app – everything is automatically erased.
  • Clear app data – go to Android Settings → Apps → Bounce Master → Storage → Clear data.
🎮 Gameplay reminder: Tap to launch the ball, drag left/right to move the paddle. Each successful bounce increases the ball’s speed. How long can you survive?

This policy may be updated if future versions introduce new features that require different data handling. The most current version will always be posted in the app’s store listing or available upon request via email.
