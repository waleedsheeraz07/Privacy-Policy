❌⭕ Privacy Policy

Tic Tac Toe  •  Effective Date: 03/04/2026

Tic Tac Toe is a simple offline multiplayer game designed for two players sharing the same device. It focuses on quick, fun gameplay without any unnecessary complexity — and without collecting your data.

🔒 This game is completely offline. We do not collect, store, or share any personal or gameplay data.

Information Collection and Use

Tic Tac Toe does not collect or store any data. There are no accounts, no tracking, and no saved game history.

  • No personal information is collected
  • No gameplay data or match history is stored
  • No analytics or tracking tools are used
  • No internet connection is required

Offline Gameplay

This app is built for local multiplayer — meaning two players play on the same device. All game logic runs entirely on your device with no external communication.

App Permissions

Tic Tac Toe requires no permissions to operate. It does not access your storage, contacts, location, microphone, or any other system features.

Third-Party Services

The app does not use any third-party services such as ads, analytics, or external SDKs. Your gameplay experience is completely private and self-contained.

Children’s Privacy

Since no data is collected, the app is safe for users of all ages and complies with privacy regulations including COPPA and GDPR standards.

Data Security

With zero data collection, there is no risk of data misuse, leaks, or unauthorized access. Everything happens locally on your device.

Data Retention

Tic Tac Toe does not retain any data. Each game session is temporary and resets when the app is closed or the reset button is used.

💡 Fresh start every time: No saved games, no history — just play and enjoy.

Data Deletion

Since no data is stored, there is nothing to delete. Closing or uninstalling the app removes all temporary data automatically.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

If the app evolves in the future, this policy may be updated. Any changes will appear here with a revised effective date.
