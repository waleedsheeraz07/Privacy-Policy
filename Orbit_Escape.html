🌌 Orbit Escape

Privacy Policy  •  Effective Date: 26 May 2026

Orbit Escape is a fast‑paced reflex game where you guide a glowing ball around a central orbit, tap to reverse direction, and avoid incoming obstacles. The app is designed with your privacy as the highest priority: no data is ever collected, stored, or shared.

🔒 Zero data collection – Orbit Escape does not collect, store, or share any personal or usage information. Everything stays on your device.

Information Collection and Use

Orbit Escape is completely offline and does not collect:

  • Personal information (name, email, location, IP address)
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Gameplay analytics, crash reports, or performance data
  • Any data sent to external servers

Local Data Storage

The app stores only one piece of information locally on your device:

  • Your highest score – saved using Android SharedPreferences

This data is used solely to display your personal best on the game screen. It never leaves your device and is automatically deleted when you uninstall the app.

Background Music

Orbit Escape includes a looping background music track (10‑minute audio file) stored inside the app package. No streaming, no downloads, no external connections. The music plays locally and stops when the app is paused or closed.

App Permissions

Orbit Escape requires no special permissions. It does not request access to storage, location, contacts, camera, microphone, or internet.

Third‑Party Services

This app contains no advertisements, no analytics, and no third‑party SDKs (such as Firebase, AdMob, Facebook, or any crash reporting tool). Everything is self‑contained in the installed application.

Children’s Privacy

Because Orbit Escape collects no personal data, it is safe for users of all ages. The app complies with the principles of COPPA (Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act) and GDPR.

Data Security

With no data collected, there is no risk of data breaches, leaks, or unauthorised access. Your high score is stored in a private app‑only directory that no other app can read.

Data Deletion

You can delete all locally stored data (your high score) in two ways:

  • Uninstall the app – everything is removed.
  • Clear app data from Android settings → Apps → Orbit Escape → Storage → Clear data.
🎮 Gameplay reminder: Tap the screen to reverse the ball’s orbit direction. Avoid colourful obstacles moving toward the centre. Each obstacle that passes the centre gives you +1 point. Speed increases with your score – how long can you survive?

This policy may be updated if new features or data handling practices are introduced in future versions. The latest version will always be available in the app’s listing or on request.
