🌀 EDGE ESCAPE

Privacy Policy  •  Effective Date: 29/04/2026

Edge Escape is a fast‑paced arcade reflex game. A glowing dot moves across the screen. Tap anywhere to instantly change its direction toward your finger. Survive as long as possible without hitting the screen edges. The longer you survive, the faster the dot moves – up to 5× the starting speed. The game is designed to be completely private — no tracking, no data collection, no internet required.

🛡️ Zero data collection — This app never collects, stores, or shares any personal or gameplay information externally. Your best (longest survival) time stays only on your device.

Information Collection and Use

Edge Escape does not collect any data. There are no analytics SDKs, no crash reporting tools, no advertising frameworks, and no internet permission. The app works fully offline.

  • No personal information (name, email, device ID, location, etc.) is collected.
  • Your current score (survival time in seconds) and your best (high) score are saved only locally on your device using Android SharedPreferences.
  • No scores, gameplay data, or any other information are ever sent to any server or third party.
  • No network access is required or used — the game runs 100% offline.

Local Storage (SharedPreferences)

The game stores exactly one piece of data on your device: your best score (the longest time you have survived without hitting an edge). This data is only used to display your personal best on the game over screen. Uninstalling the app removes this data automatically.

Gameplay Overview

When you start a new game, a glowing cyan dot appears in the centre of the screen and begins moving in a random direction. Your goal is to avoid the screen edges – touching any edge ends the game immediately.

How to play:

  • Tap anywhere on the screen → the dot instantly changes direction and flies toward your finger.
  • Each second you survive adds 1 point to your score.
  • The dot's speed increases gradually over time (by about 4.5% per second) until it reaches a maximum of 5× the starting speed.
  • A fading trail follows the dot, and the background cycles through smooth, shifting colours.
  • When you hit an edge, the screen shakes, particle effects explode, and the game ends.

All game logic (movement, collision detection, speed scaling, visual effects) runs locally on your device. No information about your taps, survival time, or performance leaves your device.

App Permissions

Edge Escape requires no special permissions. It does not access the internet, storage (beyond its own private SharedPreferences for high score), camera, microphone, contacts, or location. The game only draws on the screen and registers touch events.

Third-Party Services

This app uses no third‑party libraries that collect data. No ads, no analytics, no crash reporters. The only external code is the standard Android SDK and AppCompat (both required to run the app).

Children’s Privacy

Because no data is collected in any form, Edge Escape is safe for children of all ages. It fully complies with COPPA, GDPR‑K, and other child privacy regulations. The game contains no in‑app purchases, no chat, no user‑generated content, and no external links.

Data Security

With zero data transmission or storage outside your device, there is no risk of data breaches, leaks, or unauthorised access. Your best score is stored using Android’s standard secure local storage (SharedPreferences), accessible only by the app itself.

Data Retention and Deletion

No data is retained beyond your device’s local storage. To delete all app‑related data (including your best score):

  • Go to Android Settings → Apps → Edge Escape → Storage → Clear Data, or
  • Simply uninstall the app.

After uninstallation, no trace of your gameplay remains.
