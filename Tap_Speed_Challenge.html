⚡ Privacy Policy

Tap Speed Challenge  •  Effective Date: 04/04/2026

Tap Speed Challenge is a fast-paced tapping game designed for quick reflex testing. It delivers a simple, focused experience — no distractions, no tracking, no data collection.

🔒 This app is 100% offline. We do not collect, store, or share any personal or gameplay data.

Information Collection and Use

Tap Speed Challenge does not collect or store any data.

  • No personal information is collected
  • No tap scores or gameplay data are saved externally
  • No analytics or tracking tools are used
  • No internet connection is required

Gameplay

The game runs entirely on your device. You tap as fast as possible within a set time (e.g., 60 seconds), and your score is calculated instantly without any external processing.

App Permissions

Tap Speed Challenge requires no permissions. It does not access storage, location, contacts, microphone, or any system data.

Third-Party Services

This app does not use ads, analytics, or any third-party SDKs. Your experience is completely private and self-contained.

Children’s Privacy

Since no data is collected, Tap Speed Challenge is safe for users of all ages and aligns with privacy standards such as COPPA and GDPR.

Data Security

With zero data collection, there is no risk of data leaks or misuse. Everything happens locally on your device.

Data Retention

No data is stored. Each game session is temporary and resets after completion or when the app is closed.

⚡ Pure gameplay: Tap, score, repeat — nothing is stored, nothing is tracked.

Data Deletion

Since no data is collected or stored, there is nothing to delete. Uninstalling the app removes everything instantly.
